# Task Definition — MarketMate AI

## 1. Domain

Digital marketing for small businesses, startups and early-stage brands.

## 2. Problem statement

Small businesses need to communicate value clearly and consistently, but may lack dedicated marketing expertise or the time to plan every piece of content. Generic content generation can be vague, overly promotional or poorly matched to a specific audience.

## 3. Proposed task

Given a business brief and a marketing objective, produce a structured, audience-aware marketing recommendation or content draft that follows the requested brand voice and platform constraints.

## 4. Primary input

A request may contain:

- Business or brand description
- Product or service details
- Target audience, if known
- Marketing objective
- Platform or channel
- Brand voice or tone
- Offer, campaign dates or constraints
- Requested output format

The assistant should identify missing information when it materially affects the answer rather than inventing details.

## 5. Expected output

Depending on the request, the response may include:

1. Restatement of the marketing objective
2. Target audience and relevant customer need
3. Recommended message or marketing angle
4. Content concept or campaign direction
5. Draft copy, such as a hook, caption or product description
6. Appropriate call to action
7. Assumptions or information that needs confirmation

Not every response needs every section. The output should match the user's request.

## 6. Core task categories

- Audience and customer-persona analysis
- Brand positioning and value proposition
- Brand voice adaptation
- Social-media content planning
- Hooks, captions and calls to action
- Product and service messaging
- Campaign concept development
- Marketing strategy recommendations

## 7. Non-goals

The first version will not:

- Publish content to social platforms
- Manage advertising accounts or spend money
- Guarantee campaign performance
- Claim access to live platform trends or analytics
- Invent customer testimonials, product features or performance statistics
- Replace legal, regulatory or platform-policy review

## 8. Intended users

Small-business owners, startup founders, solo entrepreneurs and people responsible for social-media or marketing tasks.

## 9. Fine-tuning hypothesis

A carefully designed instruction dataset may help the base model follow a consistent marketing workflow and produce more structured, audience-aware outputs.

This is a hypothesis to test, not an assumed result. Fine-tuning should not be used as a substitute for providing accurate business facts or up-to-date information.

## 10. Initial success criteria

A good answer should:

- Address the stated objective
- Be appropriate for the intended audience
- Use the supplied brand voice
- Respect platform and length constraints
- Be specific rather than generic
- Avoid unsupported factual or performance claims
- Clearly flag important missing information

## 11. Example task brief

**Brief:** A small clothing brand sells oversized T-shirts to university students and wants to introduce a new collection on Instagram.

**Expected direction:** Identify a plausible audience and campaign angle, suggest an appropriate Reel or carousel concept, draft a caption and CTA, and clearly label any assumptions. Do not invent price, fabric, stock levels or customer results.
